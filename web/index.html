04 / METHOD

How the finder works

01

Parse GENCODE

Genes, transcripts and exons are extracted from the GENCODE human annotation.

02

Build introns

Introns are reconstructed from consecutive exon boundaries within transcripts.

03

Find containment

Gene intervals are compared with intron intervals on the same chromosome.

04

Validate

Relationships are checked for coordinate validity and duplicate host–nested pairs.

Scientific framing: These relationships describe computational genomic-coordinate containment. They do not by themselves establish functional regulation, evolutionary interaction, or biological causality.
